HUCISA is the student association for computing and informatics at [Haramaya University](https://www.hucisa.site). We are a community of students, alumni, mentors, and partners working so that tech-enthusiast students can build real skills, find opportunities, and start creating their own — while they are still on campus.

This GitHub organization is where that work lives: shared projects, resources, and collaboration for members of the association.

## Our vision

**From Campus to Career.**

We want students of the College of Computing and Informatics, and every tech-enthusiast student around them, to secure a tech job they care about or start a business of their own before they leave university.

University should be a launchpad: a place to discover what you care about, build skills that hold up outside the classroom, and meet people who are building the same future. Developers, designers, explorers, and anyone simply curious about technology all have a place here.

No one builds that alone. Through HUCISA, and with partners such as EEUTC, we are building a community of students, mentors, alumni, and college staff who create opportunities instead of waiting for them.

## Three mission pillars

### Skill development and mentorship
Members get practical, in-demand skills through collaborative projects, focused training, and ongoing mentorship. The association connects you with senior students, alumni, developers, founders, and industry experts on and off campus — whether you are just starting or going deeper into a specialty.

### Partnerships and internships
Partnerships run on three levels:

- **Campus.** We work with 25+ clubs and associations at Haramaya University, helping them grow through tech support, media, and design.
- **Universities.** We connect with tech communities at other Ethiopian universities, sharing knowledge, ideas, and opportunities across a national student network.
- **Beyond campus.** We collaborate with startups, tech companies, innovation hubs, and developers in Ethiopia and beyond. Those relationships open internship and professional experience while students are still in university.

### Entrepreneurship and innovation
Workshops, startup programs, and sessions with founders help students turn ideas into something real. Hackathons, innovation labs, and pitch events are where members practice launching a product, solving a local problem with technology, or leading a team.

## Leadership

The association is led by **21 positions**: a president, four higher executives, eight team heads, and eight associate heads. See the full structure on the [structure page](https://www.hucisa.site/structure).

- **President** — vision and strategy
- **Vice President** — operations and support
- **Secretary** — documentation and communication
- **Treasurer** — finance and partnerships
- **Team Leader** — coordination across teams

## Core values

- **Community first.** No one builds anything great alone. We support, inspire, and grow as one group.
- **Action over words.** Projects, events, and collaborations are how dreams become impact.
- **Growth mindset.** From beginner to specialist, every challenge is a chance to level up.
- **Innovation and creativity.** Startups and side projects both belong here.
- **Mentorship and support.** Seniors guide juniors, peers help peers, and everyone has someone to lean on.
- **Excellence and impact.** The work is for a better future, not only for a grade.
